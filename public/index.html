FAQ

Questions

How does the free trial work?

Download and install Dallar, and every feature works for 14 days. No account or card needed. When the trial ends, Dallar asks for a license key. Your data stays where it is.

How do I get a license key?

Click "Buy a license" and pay $5/month through our checkout (run by Polar). Your key appears on the receipt and in your email. Paste it into Dallar and you're done.

I have two computers. Do I need two licenses?

Yes, each license works on one computer at a time. If you switch computers, open Tools → License, click "Free this laptop", and enter the same key on the new one.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your computer. Dallar has no cloud account and we never see your tasks, payments, logins, cards or contacts. Dallar goes online to check your license key and, only if you set them up, to read your Google Calendar and save backups to your own cloud.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Dallar checks your license now and then, and keeps working for 7 days without a connection.

How do I cancel?

Cancel anytime from the link in your purchase email (the Polar customer portal). Dallar keeps working until the end of the month you paid for.

Do the hotkeys work when Dallar is hidden?

Yes. Hotkeys work from any app, whether Dallar is open, minimized or hidden in the tray. If a password lock is on, Dallar asks for it first.

Is my data backed up?

If you turn it on. Backups are encrypted on your computer before they're saved to a folder or sent to your own cloud storage (Nextcloud or any WebDAV server). To move to a new computer, enter the same cloud details and restore.

Which systems are supported?

Windows 10 and Windows 11.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC" when I install it.

That warning appears for apps from new publishers. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
