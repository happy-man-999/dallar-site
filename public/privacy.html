Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Dallar is a desktop app. The short version: your data stays on your computer. We have no account system and no server that stores what you put into the app.

Data you put into the app

Tasks, payments, contacts, notes, interview details and everything else you enter is saved in a file on your own computer. It is never sent to us. If you turn on backups, copies go only to the folder or cloud storage you choose.

When the app connects to the internet

  • License check. To check your license, the app sends your license key and your computer's name to our payment provider, Polar. This happens when you activate a key and every few hours after.
  • Google Calendar (optional). If you connect a calendar, the app reads your events directly from Google with read-only access. The connection goes from your computer to Google; we never see your calendar.
  • Downloads for optional tools. The live captions tool downloads its speech model the first time you use it. Speech is processed on your computer, not sent anywhere.
  • Small lookups. The app may fetch a website's icon when you save that website, and may check the current time against a public web server so two-factor codes come out right.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Polar, which acts as the merchant of record. Polar collects your name, email, country and payment details to process the payment and handle tax. We receive your email and order details from Polar so we can support you. We never see your full card details. See Polar's privacy policy.

This website

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.
